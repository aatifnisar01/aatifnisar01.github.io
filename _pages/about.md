I am a second year PhD student at `Virginia Tech`, working with Prof. [Shaddi Hasan](https://www.shaddih.com/). My research lies at the intersection of  `Information and Communication Technologies for Development (ICTD)`, `Internet Measurement`, `Remote Sensing`, and `AI/ML`.

My current research is in Internet measurement across the USA. I am working on DiDo, a causal inference-based framework to audit upgrade claims made in the National Broadband Map (NBM). I am also building the Broadband Surveyor Tool (BBS) to systematically measure service availability and affordability.

I use my remote sensing and AI/ML skills to answer similar research questions. I have worked on building spatio-temporal representations of ISP footprints using remote sensing data, network measurements, and deployment data. I am also involved in more projects involving remote sensing and AI/ML, and will post about them here soon.


More broadly, I am interested in building <span class="playcard" tabindex="0" aria-describedby="playcard-appropriate">appropriate<span id="playcard-appropriate" class="playcard-tooltip" role="tooltip">Appropriate means that technologies should be designed keeping the social context in mind - what material is available locally, how tools can be repaired, who will use them, and how.</span></span> technologies to address socioeconomic inequalities and the digital divide. These research interests are shaped by my past research experiences.

I worked as a Project Scientist with Prof. [Aaditeshwar Seth](https://www.cse.iitd.ac.in/~aseth/) in the ICTD Lab at IIT Delhi, where I worked on 1. Methodology to Assess Equity in Access to Water in Villages in India 2. Predicting ponds, wells, and plantation trees using high-resolution Google Maps imagery, and 3. Using Satellite Images to Track Relative Socioeconomic Development in Rural India.

Before that I was a project scientist in the [Vision Lab](https://vision-iitd.github.io/people/) of IIT Delhi with Prof. [Nandana Sengupta](https://sites.google.com/site/nandanasenguptahome/home) and Prof. [Chetan Arora](https://www.cse.iitd.ernet.in/~chetan/) working on *Assessing the Feasibility and Ethics of Economic Status Prediction using Deep Learning on Household Images*. I also worked as a research assistant at Ashoka University with Prof. [Anirban Sen](https://sites.google.com/ashoka.edu.in/anirbansen/home?authuser=0) on *PolicyStory: Leveraging Large Language Models to Generate Comprehensible Summaries of Policy-News in India*. I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science from Delhi University and my master’s degree in Computer Science from South Asian University. During my master’s, I worked with Prof. [Reshma Rastogi](https://sau.int/faculty/reshma-rastogi/) on *MLGAN: Addressing Imbalance in Multilabel Learning Using Generative Adversarial Networks*.
